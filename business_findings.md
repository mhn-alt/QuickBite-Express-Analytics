# QuickBite Express — Business Findings

## 1. Project Overview

QuickBite Express is a fictional Bengaluru-based food-tech startup used to analyze business performance and investigate a major business downturn in June 2025.

The analysis covers January to September 2025 and focuses on sales, customer behavior, restaurant performance, delivery operations, and customer satisfaction.

## 2. Overall Business Performance

| KPI | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Orders | 149,166 |
| Completed Orders | 138,054 |
| Total Revenue | ₹48.56 Million |
| Average Order Value | ₹351.75 |
| Total Customers | Approximately 105K |
| Cancellation Rate | 7.45% |
| Average Customer Rating | 4.05 |
| Average Sentiment Score | 0.53 |
| Completed-order SLA Breach Rate | 63.46% |

## 3. June 2025 Crisis Analysis

A significant decline in business performance occurred between May and June 2025.

| Metric | May 2025 | June 2025 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Orders | Approximately 23K | Approximately 9K | -58.86% |
| Revenue | ₹7.45M | ₹2.89M | -61.22% |
| Average Rating | 4.49 | 2.58 | -1.91 points |
| Customer Sentiment | 0.75 | -0.21 | -0.96 points |
| Cancellation Rate | Approximately 6% | Approximately 12% | Nearly doubled |

### Key Observations

- Order volume dropped sharply in June 2025.
- Revenue declined significantly alongside the reduction in orders.
- Customer ratings experienced a major decline.
- Customer sentiment shifted from positive to negative.
- Cancellation rates increased during the same period.
- Business performance remained below pre-crisis levels through September.

These trends coincide with the fictional food-safety incident and monsoon-related delivery disruptions. They indicate a potential relationship, but do not independently establish causation.

## 4. Customer & Revenue Insights

- Organic acquisition generated the highest revenue among the acquisition channels.
- Organic customers also contributed the largest share of orders.
- Customer order trends showed a sharp decline beginning in June.
- Customer ratings deteriorated significantly after May 2025.

**Business implication:** Customer retention, service quality, and customer trust should be priorities before increasing acquisition spending.

## 5. Restaurant & Product Insights

- Restaurant partners contributed approximately 70.3% of orders.
- Cloud kitchens contributed approximately 29.7%.
- North Indian cuisine generated the highest revenue among the displayed cuisine categories.
- Beverages and starters were the leading menu categories by item revenue.
- The top-performing restaurants contributed substantially to restaurant-level revenue.

**Business implication:** Focus on maintaining strong restaurant partnerships, identifying successful menu categories, and supporting underperforming restaurants.

## 6. Delivery & Customer Experience Insights

- Average actual delivery time was approximately 44.4 minutes.
- Average expected delivery time was approximately 38.7 minutes.
- Average delivery delay was approximately 5.7 minutes.
- Completed-order SLA breach rate was 63.46%.
- The monthly SLA breach trend increased sharply from June onward.
- Customer ratings and sentiment declined during the same period.

**Business implication:** Delivery reliability and customer experience require immediate attention.

## 7. Business Recommendations

### Operations
- Identify delivery zones and time periods with frequent delays.
- Monitor actual versus expected delivery time.
- Improve delivery partner allocation and route efficiency.

### Customer Experience
- Investigate negative customer reviews.
- Introduce customer recovery and complaint-resolution initiatives.
- Monitor ratings and sentiment after improvements.

### Restaurant Partnerships
- Strengthen food-safety standards.
- Review restaurant preparation times and performance.
- Support restaurants with declining order volumes.

### Marketing
- Prioritize customer retention campaigns.
- Use acquisition-channel performance to optimize marketing spending.
- Rebuild customer trust through transparent communication.

### Management
- Monitor revenue, orders, cancellations, and SLA performance regularly.
- Establish measurable recovery targets.
- Evaluate performance through monthly dashboard reviews.

## 8. Conclusion

The QuickBite Express analysis demonstrates how Power BI can transform business data into actionable insights.

The project identified a significant June 2025 decline in orders, revenue, customer ratings, and delivery performance. The four-page dashboard provides stakeholders with a consolidated view of business health and highlights areas requiring operational improvement.

The analysis demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, data modeling, DAX, KPI development, data visualization, and business intelligence.
